# ArduinoSemesterAssignment

**Concept: what your object does and for whom (max. 150 words).**  

My project is a game to test your reaction time. It is made for anyone who is looking to test their movement and reaction time or simply play a fun and engaging game.
My game will test your reaction speed by pressing a button. When you start the game, you will need to pull your hands back from the game board with the buttons and LEDs. Once the game has registered you far enough away, it will turn on a green light, then a yellow light, then a red light; then a random amount of time will pass (1-3 seconds), and after this happens, all three lights will turn off. When all three lights turn off, you will need to press the button as quickly as possible, and a time will be recorded. If you press the button or get too close to the button before the lights turn off, it will register as too close and stop the current round.

There will be three game modes:
1: A practice game mode; you will be able to play the game as many times as you would like. The times will display on the screen after each round, but will not be recorded.
2: A Normal mode; the game continues as usual, recording the times after each round and adding to a graph to show the rate of change.
3: A Hard mode; the game continues as the Normal mode would, but you need to be at least 1.5 meters away for the game to start.

**Which live statistics you calculate and why they are useful for your user.**  
The amount of time between the lights turning off and the button being pressed will be recorded. The practice mode lets you get a feel for the game without affecting your scores; then the Normal mode and Hard mode will record your scores and input them in a table so that you can see how much you improve.
